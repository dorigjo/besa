SEPARATE TRUST DOMAINS

Trust boundaries

  1. 01
    Identity provider

    Authenticates a principal or workload. BESA does not establish this identity.

    UPSTREAM
  2. 02
    Authority normalizer

    Verifies upstream claims and signs a bounded authority assertion.

    TRUSTED ADAPTER
  3. 03
    Admission issuer

    Binds authority, exact action, policy, audience, validity and nonce.

    SIGNING KEY
  4. 04
    Protected executor

    Independently verifies admission and consumes enforced replay state before the callback.

    SIDE-EFFECT BOUNDARY
  5. 05
    Evidence recorder

    Signs the supplied result and links it to the admitted action.

    RECORDER KEY
  6. 06
    Independent verifier

    Checks signatures and configured trust material without the issuer's private key.

    PUBLIC TRUST

A valid admission receipt does not prove execution. Signed result evidence proves what a trusted recorder supplied, not that an external system performed the real-world side effect.
